DBMS - Assignment 1
SQL Queries

Consider the given database schema:

employee (employee-name, street, city)
works (employee-name, company-name, salary)
company (company-name, city)
manages (employee-name, manager-name)

Write SQL queries for the following:

1. Find the names of all employees who work for First Bank Corporation.
2. Find the names and cities of residence of all employees who work for First Bank Corporation.
3. Find the names, street addresses, and cities of residence of all employees who work for First Bank Corporation and earn more than Rs. 10,000.
4. Find all employees in the database who live in the same city as the companies for which they work.
5. Find all employees in the database who live in the same cities and on the same streets as do their managers.
6. Find all employees in the database who do not work for First Bank Corporation.
7. Find all employees in the database who earn more than each employee of Small Bank Corporation.
8. Assume that the companies may be located in several cities. Find all companies located in every city in which Small Bank Corporation is located.
9. Find all employees who earn more than the average salary of all employees of their company.
10. Find the company that has the most employees.
11. Find the company that has the smallest payroll.
12. Find those companies whose employees earn a higher salary, on average, than the average salary at First Bank Corporation.

SQL Solutions

The queries below use standard SQL. Double quotes delimit column names
containing hyphens, such as "employee-name".

1. Employees who work for First Bank Corporation:

SELECT DISTINCT "employee-name"
FROM works
WHERE "company-name" = 'First Bank Corporation';

2. Names and residence cities of those employees:

SELECT DISTINCT e."employee-name", e.city
FROM employee AS e
JOIN works AS w ON w."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
WHERE w."company-name" = 'First Bank Corporation';

3. Names, streets, and cities of employees earning more than Rs. 10,000
at First Bank Corporation:

SELECT DISTINCT e."employee-name", e.street, e.city
FROM employee AS e
JOIN works AS w ON w."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
WHERE w."company-name" = 'First Bank Corporation'
  AND w.salary > 10000;

4. Employees who live in the same city as a company for which they work:

SELECT DISTINCT e.*
FROM employee AS e
JOIN works AS w ON w."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
JOIN company AS c ON c."company-name" = w."company-name"
WHERE e.city = c.city;

5. Employees who live on the same street and in the same city as their
managers:

SELECT DISTINCT e.*
FROM employee AS e
JOIN manages AS m ON m."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
JOIN employee AS manager
  ON manager."employee-name" = m."manager-name"
WHERE e.street = manager.street
  AND e.city = manager.city;

6. Employees who do not work for First Bank Corporation:

SELECT e.*
FROM employee AS e
WHERE NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM works AS w
    WHERE w."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
      AND w."company-name" = 'First Bank Corporation'
);

7. Employees who earn more than every employee of Small Bank Corporation:

SELECT DISTINCT e.*
FROM employee AS e
JOIN works AS w ON w."employee-name" = e."employee-name"
WHERE w.salary > ALL (
    SELECT small_bank.salary
    FROM works AS small_bank
    WHERE small_bank."company-name" = 'Small Bank Corporation'
);

8. Companies located in every city where Small Bank Corporation is located:

SELECT DISTINCT c."company-name"
FROM company AS c
WHERE NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM company AS small_bank
    WHERE small_bank."company-name" = 'Small Bank Corporation'
      AND NOT EXISTS (
          SELECT 1
          FROM company AS candidate
          WHERE candidate."company-name" = c."company-name"
            AND candidate.city = small_bank.city
      )
);

9. Employees who earn more than the average salary at their company:

SELECT DISTINCT w."employee-name"
FROM works AS w
WHERE w.salary > (
    SELECT AVG(w2.salary)
    FROM works AS w2
    WHERE w2."company-name" = w."company-name"
);

10. Company or companies with the most employees (including ties):

WITH employee_counts AS (
    SELECT "company-name", COUNT(DISTINCT "employee-name") AS employee_count
    FROM works
    GROUP BY "company-name"
)
SELECT "company-name"
FROM employee_counts
WHERE employee_count = (SELECT MAX(employee_count) FROM employee_counts);

11. Company or companies with the smallest total payroll (including ties):

WITH company_payrolls AS (
    SELECT "company-name", SUM(salary) AS payroll
    FROM works
    GROUP BY "company-name"
)
SELECT "company-name"
FROM company_payrolls
WHERE payroll = (SELECT MIN(payroll) FROM company_payrolls);

12. Companies whose average salary is higher than First Bank Corporation's
average salary:

SELECT "company-name"
FROM works
GROUP BY "company-name"
HAVING AVG(salary) > (
    SELECT AVG(salary)
    FROM works
    WHERE "company-name" = 'First Bank Corporation'
);
